# Pajajaran Physical Society (PPS)

**Physics outreach in Indonesia. Research collaboration through code.**

## What is PPS?

Pajajaran Physical Society is an independent, not-for-profit community in Indonesia. We share physics with the public and show how it helps us understand everyday phenomena and technology.

## Why PPS exists

We want more people to have the chance to learn, discuss, and develop physics. PPS introduces physics through public outreach, supports learning through education and training, and gives students and researchers a place to discuss ideas and develop their work. Follow PPS on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/pps_society/), [TikTok](https://tiktok.com/@pps_society), and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCZ_E0SyCi2waX7vSb8LZ2xg).

## What we do on GitHub

**PPS-Code-Dev** brings students, researchers, and developers together to work on physics through code. Our focus is theoretical and computational physics, including mathematical modeling, numerical methods, simulations, and scientific software.

We use these methods to ask what physical mechanisms could explain a measurement. For established experiments, we aim to reproduce key observations and test where a model holds. For new experiments, we aim to make testable predictions and help decide what to measure. We document the assumptions, parameters, and steps behind a calculation so others can reproduce it and compare it with data.

## Collaborate with us

Have a physics question, an experimental result to model, or a numerical method to try? [Open an issue in this profile repository](https://github.com/PPS-Code-Dev/PPS-Code-Dev/issues) to start a conversation. If you are working on an existing project, you can contribute a reproducible example, a benchmark, documentation, or a code change through that project's issues and pull requests.
